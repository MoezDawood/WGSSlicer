# 🚀 Deploy Your App Now - Quick Steps

## Step 1: Go to Streamlit Cloud
Open this URL in your browser:
**https://share.streamlit.io/**

## Step 2: Sign In
- Click **"Continue to sign-in"** or **"Sign in"** button
- Sign in with your **GitHub account** (same account that owns WGSSlicer repo)

## Step 3: Create New App
After signing in, you'll see your dashboard. Then:

1. Click the **"New app"** button (usually a big button in the center or top right)
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Repository**: Select `MoezDawood/WGSSlicer` from the dropdown
   - **Branch**: Select `main`
   - **Main file path**: Type `WGS_Slicer_v2.py`
   - **App URL** (optional): You can leave the default or customize it

## Step 4: Deploy
1. Click the **"Deploy"** button
2. Wait 1-2 minutes for deployment
3. You'll see a progress indicator

## Step 5: Access Your Website
Once deployed, you'll see:
- ✅ A success message
- 🔗 Your app URL: `https://[your-app-name].streamlit.app`
- Click the URL or "View app" button to open it!

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

**Can't find "New app" button?**
- Look for a "+" icon or "Create app" link
- Make sure you're signed in

**Repository not showing?**
- Make sure you pushed to GitHub successfully
- Check that the repo is public (or you've granted Streamlit Cloud access)

**Deployment fails?**
- Check the logs in the deployment view
- Verify `WGS_Slicer_v2.py` exists in the root of your repo
- Make sure `requirements.txt` is present
